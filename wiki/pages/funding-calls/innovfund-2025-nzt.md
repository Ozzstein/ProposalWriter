# Innovation Fund 2025 — Net Zero Technologies: Clean Tech Manufacturing

## Overview
EUR 1 billion topic under the Innovation Fund 2025 call, targeting construction and operation of manufacturing facilities for net-zero technology components including battery materials. Managed by CINEA. Application form V5.0.

## Key parameters
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Budget** | EUR 1,000,000,000 |
| **Full call budget** | EUR 2,900,000,000 (may increase 20%) |
| **Funding rate** | 60% of relevant costs |
| **Min CAPEX** | EUR 2,500,000 |
| **Page limit (Part B)** | 70 pages |
| **Feasibility study** | 60 pages |
| **Deadline** | 23 April 2026, 17:00 CET |

## Eligibility
- Manufacturing facilities for components of: renewables, electrolysers/fuel cells, energy storage (batteries), heat pumps
- Components include materials (except mining) that are significant factor in performance/cost
- Battery-grade cathode materials explicitly eligible (Section 1.3.2 example)
- Recycling/reusing critical materials also eligible
- Project must NOT have reached financial close at submission

## Scoring criteria
| Criterion | Max points | Weight | Weighted max | Min pass |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-------------|----------|
| Degree of innovation | 15 | 2x | 30 | 9 |
| GHG emission avoidance | 12 | 1x | 12 | — |
| Project maturity | 15 | 2x | 30 | — |
| Replicability | 15 | 1x | 15 | — |
| Cost efficiency | 15 | 1x | 15 | — |
| Bonus points | 3 | 1x | 3 | — |
| **Total** | | | **105** | |

## Minimum requirements
- Relative GHG avoidance: ≥50%
- Cost efficiency ratio: ≤EUR 200/tCO2

## Innovation must demonstrate
- Products: lower cost, higher performance, energy efficiency, durability, flexibility
- Processes: recycled materials, efficient use of critical materials, lower carbon footprint, automation, digital technologies

## Strategic notes
- Innovation (2x weight) is the highest-scoring criterion — lead with novelty
- FREYR Battery received EUR 122M from same mechanism for LFP CAM in Finland (source: [[sources/src-025-freyr-innovation-fund]])
- GHG methodology for battery component manufacturing uses Section 1.3 + Section 4 (use-phase avoidance of BESS deployment, not factory process emissions)
