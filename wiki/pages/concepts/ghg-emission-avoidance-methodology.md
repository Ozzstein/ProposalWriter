# GHG Emission Avoidance Methodology for the Innovation Fund

## Overview
The European Innovation Fund (IF) requires applicants to calculate and report GHG emission avoidance — the difference between emissions from the proposed innovative process and a baseline representing current best available technology (BAT) or the counterfactual scenario. For LFP CAM manufacturing, the GHG avoidance calculation must quantify: (1) the baseline GHG intensity of conventional gas-fired LFP CAM production (kgCO2/kg LFP CAM), (2) the GHG intensity of the proposed electrified production at Brindisi under current and projected Italian grid carbon intensity, and (3) the total emission avoidance over the project lifetime (tonnes CO2e). Key challenges include the absence of published LFP CAM-specific energy data (GAP-003, GAP-004) and the time-varying nature of Italian grid decarbonization.

## Key findings
- Electricity consumption is 39.7% of LFP battery manufacturing GWP — the dominant decarbonization lever (source: [[sources/SRC-ED-015]])
- Italy grid (310-331 gCO2/kWh 2023-2025) currently above ~180 gCO2/kWh electrification breakeven vs gas; projected to cross ~2027-2028 (source: [[sources/SRC-ED-016]])
- Italy NECP 2030 (~146 gCO2/kWh) enables 55-70% GHG reduction for fully electrified LFP CAM at Brindisi (source: [[sources/SRC-ED-017]])
- G7 2035 near-100% renewables commitment implies 80-90% GHG reduction vs gas-fired baseline (source: [[sources/SRC-ED-013]])
- NMC analog (~4 kWh/kg, Ahmed et al. 2017) used as best available proxy for LFP CAM process energy in Innovation Fund calculation (source: [[sources/SRC-ED-011]])
- No LFP-specific industrial-scale energy or GHG data exists — primary data generation is a required project output (source: [[sources/SRC-ED-014]])
- Grid decarbonization could cut global battery supply chain GHG by up to 38% by 2050 (source: [[sources/SRC-021]])

## Open questions
- What baseline scenario does the Innovation Fund require — BAT gas-fired CAM, or Chinese grid-average LFP CAM? The choice significantly changes the avoidance calculation.
- How should the time-varying Italian grid decarbonization trajectory be reflected in the Innovation Fund calculation — lifetime average or year-by-year?
- Does the IF accept probabilistic GHG avoidance ranges (reflecting grid trajectory uncertainty) or require a point estimate?
