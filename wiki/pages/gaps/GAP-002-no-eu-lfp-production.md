# No EU LFP CAM Production Online

**Severity**: critical | **Strategic importance**: 9/10 | **Type**: application

## Description
As of late 2025, no EU-based company has brought LFP cathode active material production online at commercial scale. China controls >99% of global LFP CAM production capacity. Chinese firms including CATL are simultaneously expanding manufacturing within Europe (cell level), further deepening EU strategic dependency. FREYR's Vaasa (Finland) facility has not reached Final Investment Decision. This gap directly threatens the EU's Net-Zero Industry Act (NZIA) and Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA) strategic autonomy objectives for the battery value chain.

## Evidence
- [[sources/SRC-023]] — European battery material production survey confirming zero LFP CAM online in EU
- [[sources/SRC-024]] — CATL European expansion and EU strategic dependency analysis
- [[sources/SRC-025]] — FREYR Vaasa FID status and US capital prioritization

## Addressed by
- **faam-eni-circular-energy**: The Brindisi plant would be the first LFP CAM production facility in Italy and Southern Europe, producing up to 10,000 t/yr with a fully electrified process. The project directly fills the EU production gap and provides a replicable template for scale-up across Europe.
