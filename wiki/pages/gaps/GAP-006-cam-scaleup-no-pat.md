# CAM Scale-Up Is Semi-Empirical With No PAT Framework Implemented

**Severity**: high | **Strategic importance**: 7/10 | **Type**: technology

## Description
Industrial scale-up of battery cathode material synthesis from lab to production scale is currently semi-empirical: scale-up factors are derived from trial-and-error campaigns rather than mechanistic models. Process Analytical Technology (PAT) and Quality by Design (QbD) frameworks — well established in pharmaceutical manufacturing — enable model-based scale-up with real-time quality assurance but have not been implemented for battery CAM production. This means every new CAM production facility must independently solve scale-up problems at significant cost and time.

## Evidence
- [[sources/SRC-028]] — PAT/QbD pharmaceutical framework review and battery CAM transferability analysis
- [[sources/SRC-001]] — Battery manufacturing process control literature documenting semi-empirical scale-up practice
- [[sources/SRC-DT-REPORT]] — DT landscape report confirming PAT absence in battery CAM production

## Addressed by
- **faam-eni-circular-energy**: The project integrates PAT principles (in-line Raman, XRD, thermal imaging) with the DT-based process model to create the first QbD-compliant LFP CAM production control system. This directly addresses the semi-empirical scale-up gap by providing model-based scale-up capability transferable to future EU LFP CAM plants.
