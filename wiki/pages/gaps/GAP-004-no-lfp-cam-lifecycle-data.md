# No Lifecycle GHG Data for LFP CAM Production at Industrial Scale

**Severity**: high | **Strategic importance**: 7/10 | **Type**: research

## Description
No published lifecycle GHG emission data specific to LFP cathode active material production exists at industrial scale. All current estimates are extrapolated from NMC chemistry analogs or from cement/lime calcination processes. Systematic review of published LFP LCAs (Xie et al. 2017; Lin et al. 2024) confirms none report primary CAM synthesis energy at industrial scale. This prevents accurate EU battery passport GHG footprinting and makes the Innovation Fund emission avoidance calculation reliant on analog estimates. Derived from CLM-028.

## Evidence
- [[sources/SRC-ED-014]] — Xie et al. (2017) LFP LCA review confirming no industrial-scale CAM synthesis energy data
- [[sources/SRC-ED-015]] — Lin et al. (2024) LFP LCA confirming same gap in primary synthesis energy data
- [[sources/SRC-ED-011]] — NMC CAM analog energy data (Ahmed et al. 2017) used as best available proxy

## Addressed by
- **faam-eni-circular-energy**: The project includes dedicated lifecycle assessment work packages to generate primary GHG data for the full LFP CAM synthesis process at industrial scale. This data will be published and submitted to the Battery Passport registry, addressing both the research gap and the EU Battery Regulation compliance requirement.
