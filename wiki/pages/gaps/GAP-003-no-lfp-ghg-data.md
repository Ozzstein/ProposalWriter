# No Published GHG Data Specific to Electrified LFP Calcination

**Severity**: high | **Strategic importance**: 7/10 | **Type**: research

## Description
No published work specifically quantifies the GHG emissions of electrified LFP calcination compared to gas-fired benchmarks. Quantitative GHG data exists for electrified cement and lime calcination as analogs, but LFP-specific energy consumption data for the Innovation Fund GHG emission avoidance calculation is a data gap that must be filled by the project. This gap creates uncertainty in the Innovation Fund GHG baseline and emission avoidance calculation, requiring the project to generate primary data. Derived from CLM-021.

## Evidence
- [[sources/SRC-019]] — Electrified cement calcination GHG data used as LFP analog (gap by analogy)
- [[sources/SRC-020]] — Electrified lime calcination GHG comparison — closest published reference but not LFP-specific
- [[sources/SRC-021]] — Battery supply chain GHG modeling without LFP CAM synthesis-specific breakdown

## Addressed by
- **faam-eni-circular-energy**: The project will generate primary energy consumption and GHG emission data for electrified spray drying and calcination of LFP CAM at pilot and industrial scale, filling this data gap for the Innovation Fund baseline and providing a reference dataset for future EU LFP CAM projects.
