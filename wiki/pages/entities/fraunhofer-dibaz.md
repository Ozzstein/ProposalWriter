# Fraunhofer FFB DiBaZ Project

## Overview
DiBaZ (Digitaler Batteriezellen-Zwilling — Digital Battery Cell Twin) is a research project by Fraunhofer FFB (Forschungsfertigung Batteriezelle) in Münster, Germany. The project developed and deployed a digital twin for pilot battery cell production, covering formation cycling, cell assembly, and quality control. DiBaZ is the primary European quantitative benchmark for DT-driven yield improvement in battery cell manufacturing, reporting a 10.3% scrap rate reduction in the pilot production line.

## From: [[sources/SRC-DT-REPORT]]
DT landscape report documents DiBaZ as the Fraunhofer FFB flagship DT project: 10.3% scrap reduction in pilot battery cell production, DT scope covering formation, assembly, and quality control. Notes that DiBaZ operates at the cell manufacturing level and does not address upstream CAM or electrode precursor synthesis. Used as quantitative precedent for DT ROI in battery manufacturing.
