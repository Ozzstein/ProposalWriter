# NGK Insulators

## Overview
NGK Insulators, Ltd. is a Japanese industrial ceramics company (headquartered in Nagoya) that manufactures roller hearth kilns (RHK) and tunnel kilns used in battery cathode material calcination. NGK is the leading supplier of roller hearth kilns for LFP and NMC CAM production in China and is evaluating supply of electric roller hearth kiln systems for European battery material manufacturers. NGK's kiln technology is central to the FAAM-ENI project's calcination equipment selection.
