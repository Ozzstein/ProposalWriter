# CATL (Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited)

## Overview
CATL is the world's largest battery manufacturer by capacity, headquartered in Ningde, China. The company dominates global lithium-ion battery cell production and is a major LFP cell manufacturer. CATL is expanding into Europe with a gigafactory in Debrecen, Hungary, producing battery cells for European automotive OEMs. CATL's European expansion represents a strategic concern: European battery assembly will depend on CATL's Chinese LFP CAM supply chain unless European CAM production is established.
