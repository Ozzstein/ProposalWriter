# FIB S.p.A. (FAAM)

## Overview
FIB S.p.A. (Fabbrica Italiana Accumulatori Motocarri Montecchio), operating under the FAAM brand, is an Italian battery manufacturer headquartered in Montecchio Maggiore (Vicenza). FIB/FAAM operates industrial battery manufacturing facilities and has existing BESS capacity at its Teverola site (Teverola 1: 300 MWh operational; Teverola 2: 8 GWh planned). The Brindisi site is the proposed location for the LFP CAM production plant, leveraging existing industrial infrastructure and Southern Italy location advantages (renewable energy access, incentive zones). FIB/FAAM has no existing patents in LFP CAM synthesis (CLM-015) and is the project's lead applicant in the ESS joint venture with Eni.
