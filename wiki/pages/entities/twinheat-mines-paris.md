# TwinHeat Industrial Chair (MINES Paris)

## Overview
TwinHeat is an industrial research chair at MINES ParisTech (École des Mines de Paris), focused on developing digital twins for industrial heat treatment processes. The chair combines CFD (computational fluid dynamics) simulation, neural network surrogate models, and deep reinforcement learning (DRL) to optimize industrial furnace heating schedules in real time. TwinHeat is the closest published precedent for a DT applied to high-temperature continuous thermal processing — the domain most directly analogous to LFP calcination.

## From: [[sources/SRC-DT-REPORT]]
DT landscape report covers TwinHeat as a benchmark for DT applied to industrial furnace optimization. Documents 10-15% energy reduction in heat treatment furnaces achieved through DT-optimized heating schedules. Describes the CFD + neural network surrogate + DRL architecture that enables real-time control. Notes direct methodological transferability to LFP rotary kiln calcination: same continuous thermal process class, similar distributed temperature control problem.
