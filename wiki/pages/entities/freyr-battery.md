# FREYR Battery

## Overview
FREYR Battery is a Norwegian battery company (listed on NYSE) that received a EUR 122M European Innovation Fund grant for a 30,000 t/yr LFP CAM production facility in Vaasa, Finland. FREYR is the primary European precedent for Innovation Fund-funded LFP CAM manufacturing and the closest comparator project for the FAAM-ENI proposal. However, FREYR's Vaasa plant has not reached Final Investment Decision as of late 2025, with the company redirecting capital and management focus to its Giga America (USA) facility under US IRA incentives.
