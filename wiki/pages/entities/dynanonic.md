# Foshan Dynanonic Technology

## Overview
Foshan Dynanonic Technology Co., Ltd. is a leading Chinese LFP cathode active material producer, headquartered in Foshan, Guangdong Province. Dynanonic is one of the largest dedicated LFP CAM manufacturers globally and supplies major battery cell producers including CATL. The company exemplifies the concentrated Chinese LFP CAM production that creates the strategic dependency addressed by the FAAM-ENI project.
