# Eni S.p.A.

## Overview
Eni S.p.A. is Italy's largest energy company, a multinational integrated energy major headquartered in Rome. In the context of the FAAM-ENI Circular Energy project, Eni is a strategic partner and co-investor contributing its energy infrastructure, grid connection, and BESS deployment expertise to the Brindisi site. Eni is deploying Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) for grid stabilization and renewable energy firming, creating internal demand for LFP cells and a strategic interest in securing domestic LFP CAM supply. Eni has no existing IP in LFP CAM synthesis (CLM-015) and participates as a technology acquirer and industrial offtaker.
