# Kanthal (Sandvik Group)

## Overview
Kanthal is a Swedish manufacturer of electric heating elements and industrial heating systems, a division of the Sandvik Group. Kanthal produces resistance heating elements (Kanthal A-1, Globar SiC), electric roller hearth kilns, and high-temperature furnace systems used in battery material calcination, ceramics, and industrial powder processing. Kanthal's electric kiln technology is directly relevant to the FAAM-ENI project's electrified calcination design, and the company is a potential equipment supplier for the Brindisi plant.
