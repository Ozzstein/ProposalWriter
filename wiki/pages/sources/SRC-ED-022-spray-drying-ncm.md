# Spray-Drying for NCM Precursor synthesis

**Authors**: Park et al. (2024)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Recent research on spray drying applications for NMC precursor synthesis, demonstrating integration with downstream calcination. Study highlights wastewater elimination benefits of spray drying approach compared to conventional wet chemistry methods.

## Key findings
- Spray drying + calcination integration for NMC CAM
- Eliminates wastewater generation
- Process efficiency improvement
- Recent technology (2024)
- Applicable to LFP and other CAM formulations
