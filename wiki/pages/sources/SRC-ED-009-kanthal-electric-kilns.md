# Green batteries with electric kilns

**Authors**: Kanthal/Sandvik (2022)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Research from leading thermal equipment manufacturer Kanthal (Sandvik) demonstrating superior thermal efficiency of electric kiln technology for battery material manufacturing. The study reports 95% thermal efficiency for electric kilns compared to 25-60% efficiency for conventional gas-fired systems.

## Key findings
- Electric kiln thermal efficiency: 95%
- Conventional gas kiln efficiency: 25-60%
- Significant efficiency advantage for green manufacturing
- Applicable to CAM synthesis and sintering
- Equipment manufacturer perspective and validation
