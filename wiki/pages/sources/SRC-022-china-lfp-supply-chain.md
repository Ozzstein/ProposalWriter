# China's hold on the lithium-ion battery supply chain

**Authors**: Multiple (2025)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Analysis of global lithium-ion battery supply chain concentration, documenting that China controls >99% of global LFP cathode active material (CAM) production. Recent export controls implemented in 2025 highlight strategic vulnerabilities in European battery manufacturing independence, underscoring the urgency of EU domestic CAM production capacity.

## Key findings
- China holds >99% of global LFP cathode active material production
- Export licensing controls imposed by China (2025)
- EU lacks domestic LFP CAM manufacturing capacity
- Supply chain concentration poses strategic and economic risk to EU battery industry
