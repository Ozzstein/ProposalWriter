# Break-even analysis of HTHP for milk spray drying

**Authors**: Schlosser et al. (2023)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Economic analysis establishing break-even conditions for high-temperature heat pump deployment in spray drying. The research demonstrates that HTHPs can satisfy 60.3% of thermal demand in spray drying to 127°C, with economic break-even occurring at electricity-to-gas price ratios between 1.20 and 1.56.

## Key findings
- HTHPs viable for spray drying up to 127°C
- Can meet 60.3% of thermal demand at this temperature
- Break-even price ratios: 1.20-1.56 (electricity/gas)
- Economic viability depending on regional energy pricing
- High applicability to CAM synthesis thermal processes
