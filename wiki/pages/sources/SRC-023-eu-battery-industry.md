# Powering the EU's future: Strengthening the battery industry

**Authors**: European Parliament Research Service (2025)
**Type**: policy_document | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Official European Parliament analysis of the EU battery industry landscape and strategic gaps. Documents the absence of operational LFP cathode active material production within the EU and tracks CATL's expanding European manufacturing footprint. Identifies LFP CAM production as a critical strategic capability gap requiring urgent EU policy action under the Net Zero Industry Act (NZIA).

## Key findings
- No EU domestic LFP cathode active material production currently online
- CATL actively expanding manufacturing presence in Europe
- LFP CAM identified as critical strategic capability gap
- Net Zero Industry Act (NZIA) targets European battery supply chain autonomy
- EU battery manufacturing ecosystem remains dependent on non-EU suppliers
