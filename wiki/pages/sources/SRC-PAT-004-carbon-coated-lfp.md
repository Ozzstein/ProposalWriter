# In-situ carbon coated LFP cathode

**Authors**: ARCI India (2022)
**Type**: Patent | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
International patent WO2022144917A1 describing an alternative route for producing carbon-coated LFP cathode material using solid-state chemistry approaches. This manufacturing method differs significantly from conventional spray-drying processes, offering a distinct synthesis pathway for enhanced material properties.

## Key findings
- Solid-state synthesis route for LFP production
- In-situ carbon coating during material formation
- Non-spray-drying alternative with potential process advantages
- Recent patent filing (2022) indicates ongoing innovation in alternative synthesis methods
