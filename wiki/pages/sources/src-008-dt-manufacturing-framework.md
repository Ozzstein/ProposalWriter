# Framework for a Digital Twin in Manufacturing - Scope and Requirements

**Authors**: Shao, G. and Kibira, D. (2020)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high

## Summary
The foundational paper that motivates ISO 23247 standardization. It articulates essential requirements for industrial digital twins, emphasizing real-time bidirectional data flows, embedded simulation capabilities, and composable component architectures. This work establishes the conceptual and technical foundations underlying manufacturing DT standards.

## Key findings
- Real-time data acquisition essential for operational DTs
- Bidirectional flow required for prescriptive (control) capabilities
- Simulation must be integrated within operational twin, not separate
- Component composability enables adaptation across manufacturing contexts
- Standards needed to ensure interoperability and scalability
