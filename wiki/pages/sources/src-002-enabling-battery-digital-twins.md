# Enabling Battery Digital Twins at the Industrial Scale

**Authors**: Dubarry, M. et al. (2023)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Published in Joule, this paper makes a foundational case for adopting a holistic, standardized mathematical framework for industrial battery digital twins that spans multiple scales—from materials properties through electrochemistry to pack-level systems performance. The authors argue that interoperable standards are essential for meaningful industrial deployment.

## Key findings
- Holistic framework required to connect materials, cell, and system scales
- Standardized mathematical representation necessary for industrial adoption
- Data integration across heterogeneous manufacturing sources is critical
- Framework must enable predictive and prescriptive capabilities at industrial scale
