# Energy Consumption of Industrial Spray Dryers

**Authors**: Baker, C.G.J. et al. (2005)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Foundational research quantifying energy demands of industrial spray drying operations, establishing baseline thermal efficiency metrics. The study documents average energy consumption of 4.87 GJ/t of water evaporated, with approximately 29% of energy wasted in conventional spray drying systems. Highly cited (146 citations).

## Key findings
- Average energy consumption: 4.87 GJ/tonne water evaporated
- Approximately 29% of energy dissipated as waste heat
- 146 citations indicating foundational importance
- Establishes baseline for energy recovery and efficiency improvements
