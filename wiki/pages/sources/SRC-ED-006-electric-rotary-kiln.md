# Electric Rotary Kiln Calcination

**Authors**: Katajisto (2020)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Research assessing the feasibility and promise of electric rotary kiln technology for high-temperature calcination processes in lime and cement production. The work establishes electric kiln approaches as a viable pathway for process electrification in industrial thermal processing.

## Key findings
- Electric kiln technology viable for calcination
- Applicable to lime and cement manufacturing
- Demonstrates process electrification feasibility
- Foundation for CAM sintering electrification
