# Italy electricity carbon intensity data

**Authors**: Nowtricity/ISPRA/IEA (2025)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Current electricity carbon intensity data for Italian grid from Nowtricity, ISPRA (Italian environmental agency), and IEA. The research establishes baseline emissions at 310-331 gCO2/kWh with break-even analysis showing electrification benefits at ~180 gCO2/kWh, and NECP 2030 target of 146 gCO2/kWh.

## Key findings
- Current Italian grid: 310-331 gCO2/kWh
- Break-even threshold: ~180 gCO2/kWh for electrification
- NECP 2030 target: 146 gCO2/kWh renewable generation
- Regional context for European manufacturing
- Recent data (2025) reflecting current grid trajectory
