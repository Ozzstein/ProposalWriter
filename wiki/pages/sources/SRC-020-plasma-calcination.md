# A comparative study of conventional lime kilns and plasma calcination

**Authors**: Dylong, M. et al. (2025)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Provides comparative analysis of conventional thermal calcination versus plasma-based electrified calcination for industrial limestone processing. Plasma calcination demonstrates advantages in reaction kinetics, equipment footprint, and CO2 emissions profile, supporting the feasibility of plasma technologies for energy-intensive carbonate decomposition.

## Key findings
- Plasma calcination offers significantly faster reaction rates than conventional kilns
- Equipment volume requirements reduced by plasma approach
- CO2 emissions substantially lower with electric plasma versus fossil fuels
- Industrial-scale plasma reactors demonstrated technical viability
