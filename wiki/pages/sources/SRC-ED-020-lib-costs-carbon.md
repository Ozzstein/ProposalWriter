# Costs and carbon footprint of LIB manufacturing

**Authors**: Gutsch et al. (2024)
**Type**: Paper (Applied Energy) | **Quality**: Very High

## Summary
Comprehensive cost-benefit analysis of LIB manufacturing with focus on carbon footprint reduction strategies. Study identifies low-carbon electricity as more impactful than recycling for reducing global warming potential (GWP), with 103 citations in Applied Energy indicating high impact.

## Key findings
- Low-carbon electricity > recycling for GWP reduction
- Identifies electricity as primary leverage point
- Manufacturing cost implications of decarbonization
- 103 citations in Applied Energy
- Recent (2024) with practical cost insights
