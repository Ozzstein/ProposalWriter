# Electrified externally heated rotary calciner

**Authors**: Jacob et al. (2023)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Companion study examining externally heated electric rotary calciner designs with integrated CO2 capture capabilities. The research identifies space constraints at scale as a limiting factor while demonstrating the feasibility of combining electrified calcination with emissions capture.

## Key findings
- External heating approach for rotary calcination
- Integration of CO2 capture capability
- Space constraints at scale require careful design
- Enables simultaneous emissions reduction and material processing
- Preferred design relative to internal heating approaches
