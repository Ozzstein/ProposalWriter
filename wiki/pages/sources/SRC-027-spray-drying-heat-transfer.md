# Heat and mass transfer during spray drying of cathode material

**Authors**: Rajasekar, K. et al. (2022)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Presents volumetric heat transfer correlations and mass transfer analysis for industrial spray drying of iron phosphate cathode materials. Provides quantitative relationships for predicting drying behavior and product quality during LFP cathode active material manufacturing, supporting process design and scale-up calculations for production facilities.

## Key findings
- Volumetric heat transfer correlations developed for iron phosphate spray drying
- Heat transfer coefficient relationships enable scale-up design
- Drying kinetics modeled for industrial spray tower dimensions
- Process parameters correlate with final cathode material properties
