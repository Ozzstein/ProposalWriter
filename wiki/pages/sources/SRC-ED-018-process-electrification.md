# Process decarbonization through electrification

**Authors**: Masuku et al. (2024)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Research demonstrating decarbonization pathways through industrial process electrification, particularly for resistance heating to temperatures exceeding 1100°C. Study highlights precision advantages of electric heating (tighter temperature control) compared to combustion-based thermal processes.

## Key findings
- Electric resistance heating to >1100°C capability
- Superior temperature precision vs. combustion systems
- Decarbonization pathway for high-temperature processes
- Applicable to calcination and sintering
- Recent innovation (2024) reflecting current capabilities
