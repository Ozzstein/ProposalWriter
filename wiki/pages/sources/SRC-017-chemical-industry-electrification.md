# Decarbonization of the chemical industry through electrification

**Authors**: Mallapragada, D. et al. (2023)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high
**Citations**: 184 in Joule journal

## Summary
Demonstrates that direct electrification represents the most mature and near-term viable decarbonization pathway for the chemical industry. The analysis shows clear technical and economic advantages of electrification over alternative approaches, making it a priority strategy for industrial emissions reduction in the chemical sector.

## Key findings
- Direct electrification is the most mature near-term decarbonization pathway
- Technical solutions exist for most chemical processes at industrial scale
- Economic feasibility improves with renewable energy availability
- Industrial-scale demonstrations confirm technical readiness
