# To decarbonize industry, we must decarbonize heat

**Authors**: Thiel, G.P. and Stark, A.K. (2021)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high
**Citations**: 225 in Joule journal

## Summary
Establishes that industrial process heating accounts for approximately 20% of global CO2 emissions and represents the single broadest leverage point for industrial decarbonization. Addresses the critical but often-overlooked role of thermal energy in achieving climate targets across manufacturing sectors.

## Key findings
- Industrial process heat = ~20% of global CO2 emissions
- Thermal energy decarbonization is the most critical industrial lever
- Most industrial sectors lack mature decarbonization solutions for heat
- Both technical and policy interventions needed for widespread adoption
