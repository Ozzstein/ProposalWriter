# One-Pot CAM Precursor synthesis

**Authors**: Nano One Materials (2021)
**Type**: Patent | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
International patent WO2022047585A1 from Nano One Materials describing one-pot synthesis approaches for cathode active material (CAM) precursors, specifically targeting NMC and NCA (nickel manganese cobalt and nickel cobalt aluminum) compositions. This simplified synthesis pathway represents an alternative to multi-step precursor preparation.

## Key findings
- One-pot synthesis methodology for CAM precursor production
- Applicable to NMC and NCA formulations
- Simplified process chemistry reducing process steps
- Innovation in precursor preparation efficiency
