# Estimating environmental impacts of global lithium-ion battery supply chain

**Authors**: Llamas-Orozco, J.A. et al. (2023)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high
**Citations**: 75 in PNAS Nexus

## Summary
Comprehensive life-cycle assessment of global lithium-ion battery supply chains, demonstrating that grid decarbonization could reduce battery manufacturing emissions by 38%. Further analysis shows that LFP cathode chemistry exhibits lower GHG intensity than NMC alternatives, supporting the environmental case for LFP-based battery technology.

## Key findings
- Grid decarbonization potential: 38% reduction in battery supply chain emissions
- LFP manufacturing has lower GHG profile than NMC chemistry
- Critical raw material sourcing dominates supply chain environmental impact
- Manufacturing location and electricity grid composition are key variables
