# INNOVFUND-2025-NZT Call Fiche

**Authors**: EC/CINEA (2025)
**Type**: policy_document | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Official call documentation for the 2025 Net Zero Technology Innovation Fund budget allocation. Specifies EUR 1 billion total budget, 60% co-funding ratio for eligible projects, and confirms explicit eligibility of battery cathode active material manufacturing. Defines scoring criteria, submission requirements, and eligibility thresholds for ESS JV project proposal evaluation.

## Key findings
- Total budget: EUR 1 billion for NZT Innovation Fund (2025)
- Standard co-funding ratio: 60% EU grant, 40% beneficiary contribution
- Eligible technology: Battery cathode active material (CAM) manufacturing
- Call timeline: Open call structure with rolling deadlines
- Scoring criteria address innovation, commercial viability, and EU strategic value
