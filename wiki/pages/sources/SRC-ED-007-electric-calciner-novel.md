# Novel internally heated electric rotary calciner

**Authors**: Jacob et al. (2023)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Detailed study of a novel internally heated electric rotary calciner design for material processing. The research identified 60% heat loss in pilot-scale operation, with analysis recommending roller hearth kiln designs as preferred alternative for improved thermal efficiency at scale.

## Key findings
- 60% heat loss in pilot-scale internal heating design
- Identifies thermal efficiency limitations
- Roller hearth kiln design recommended as preferred alternative
- Important for optimizing electric calcination architecture
