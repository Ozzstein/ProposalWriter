# CFD model for NMC calcination in electric roller hearth kiln

**Authors**: Jo et al. (2025)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
First computational fluid dynamics (CFD) model specifically developed for NMC calcination in electric roller hearth kiln environments. The study demonstrates precise heater control with less than 5% temperature deviation, advancing understanding of heat transfer and temperature uniformity in electrified calcination systems.

## Key findings
- First CFD model for electric roller hearth kiln calcination
- Achieves heater control with <5% temperature deviation
- Validates precision of electrified thermal processing
- Applicable to LFP and other CAM formulations
- Recent innovation (2025) reflecting current state of practice
