# Machine Learning-Assisted Multi-Objective Optimization of Battery Manufacturing from Synthetic Data

**Authors**: Duquesnoy, M. et al. (2022)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Demonstrates the ARTISTIC platform's inverse design capability using Bayesian Optimization coupled with machine learning surrogates. The study explicitly includes LFP cathode active material alongside NMC chemistries and shows measurable manufacturing improvements—specifically scrap rate reduction—through the ML-BO optimization loop applied to electrode manufacturing parameters.

## Key findings
- Bayesian Optimization loop reduces manufacturing scrap rates
- LFP and NMC chemistries optimized simultaneously
- Synthetic data generation enables efficient surrogate model training
- Inverse design identifies non-obvious optimal parameter combinations
