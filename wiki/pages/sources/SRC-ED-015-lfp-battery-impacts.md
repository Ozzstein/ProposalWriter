# Environmental impacts of LFP batteries in China

**Authors**: Lin et al. (2024)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Recent environmental impact assessment of LFP battery production in China, quantifying contribution of electricity grid emissions. Study establishes electricity generation as 39.71% of total global warming potential (GWP), demonstrating critical importance of grid decarbonization for battery manufacturing emissions.

## Key findings
- Electricity: 39.71% of GWP for LFP battery production
- Grid decarbonization as key leverage point
- Chinese electricity mix as baseline scenario
- Recent data (2024) reflecting current conditions
- Supports urgency of electrification and clean electricity
