# High-Temperature Heat Pumps for Spray Drying

**Authors**: Tenpenny et al. (2025)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Recent research demonstrating the economic and environmental advantages of high-temperature heat pump (HTHP) systems for spray drying applications when compared to gas-based thermal systems. The analysis includes social cost of carbon accounting, showing HTHP as lower-GHG alternative.

## Key findings
- HTHP systems environmentally superior to gas heating
- Lower GHG emissions when social cost of carbon considered
- Economic viability with appropriate carbon pricing
- Applicable to batch thermal processes including spray drying
