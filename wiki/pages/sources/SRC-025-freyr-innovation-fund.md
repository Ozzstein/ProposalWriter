# FREYR Battery Selected by EU Innovation Fund

**Authors**: FREYR/EC (2024)
**Type**: project_reference | **Quality**: high

## Summary
FREYR Battery project received EUR 122 million in EU Innovation Fund grant support for development of a 30,000 tonnes per year LFP cathode active material manufacturing facility in Finland. Serves as a direct comparable project demonstrating EU-funded approach to establishing strategic battery supply chain capacity, commercial scale targets, and Innovation Fund support mechanisms.

## Key findings
- Grant amount: EUR 122 million from EU Innovation Fund
- Target production capacity: 30,000 tonnes/year LFP cathode active material
- Location: Finland (EU strategic manufacturing)
- Timeline: Commercial-scale operation planned for mid-2020s
- Demonstrates EU Innovation Fund viability for battery CAM projects
