# LCA of automotive battery cell production

**Authors**: Degen & Schütte (2021)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Very High

## Summary
Comprehensive life cycle assessment (LCA) quantifying environmental impact of automotive battery cell production with focus on manufacturing emissions. Study demonstrates 88% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions when transitioning from gas+electricity baseline to low-carbon electricity systems (4.54 to 0.53 kgCO2eq).

## Key findings
- Current pathway: 4.54 kgCO2eq per cell unit
- Low-carbon pathway: 0.53 kgCO2eq per cell unit
- 88% GHG reduction through electricity decarbonization
- Demonstrates critical importance of clean electricity
- Foundation for circular economy and green manufacturing claims
