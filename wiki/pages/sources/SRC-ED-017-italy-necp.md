# Italy NECP 2023 - National Energy and Climate Plan

**Authors**: Italian Ministry (2023)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Official Italian National Energy and Climate Plan (NECP) 2023 establishing national targets and roadmap for renewable energy deployment and decarbonization. Plan commits to 65% renewable energy generation by 2030 with 131 GW installed capacity target.

## Key findings
- 65% renewable energy target by 2030
- 131 GW renewable generation capacity target
- Supports low-carbon electricity availability
- Policy context for manufacturing location decision
- Government commitment to energy transition
