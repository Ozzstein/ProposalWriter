# Transcritical High-Temperature Heat Pumps

**Authors**: Zhao et al. (2024)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Advanced research on transcritical heat pump cycles for high-temperature applications, demonstrating coefficient of performance (COP) of 3.6 at 200°C. This technology extends the operating range of heat pump systems to higher temperatures suitable for calcination and sintering processes.

## Key findings
- Transcritical cycle enables 200°C operation
- COP 3.6 at 200°C outlet temperature
- Extends heat pump applicability to calcination processes
- Addresses higher temperature thermal demands in CAM production
