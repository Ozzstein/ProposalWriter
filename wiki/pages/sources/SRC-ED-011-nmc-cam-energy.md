# Cost and energy of NMC CAM production

**Authors**: Ahmed et al. (2017)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Comprehensive analysis of energy requirements and production costs for NMC (nickel manganese cobalt) cathode active material synthesis. The study establishes baseline energy consumption at approximately 4 kWh/kg NMC at commercial scale, with high citation count (168) indicating foundational importance.

## Key findings
- Energy consumption: ~4 kWh/kg NMC at scale
- Commercial-scale energy data
- 168 citations indicating foundational significance
- Baseline for comparing LFP and other CAM technologies
- Supports cost-benefit analysis of electrification strategies
