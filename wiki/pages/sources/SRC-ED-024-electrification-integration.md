# Sustainable process integration of electrification

**Authors**: Son et al. (2022)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Research on integrated electrification approaches for industrial chemical processes, demonstrating economic viability of combined heat pump and electrode boiler technologies. Study shows feasibility at 23% electricity cost reduction, providing cost-benefit framework for decarbonization strategy.

## Key findings
- Heat pump + electrode boiler integration viable
- Economics: feasible at 23% electricity cost reduction
- Sustainable process integration framework
- Applicable to multi-step thermal processes
- Practical cost-benefit analysis
