# Beyond the Limits of Lithium Iron Phosphate

**Authors**: Kakarla, A.K. et al. (2025)
**Type**: review | **Quality**: high

## Summary
A 2025 review identifying AI and machine learning-driven optimization as a transformative approach for advancing LFP battery technology. The work catalogs current limitations of LFP and argues that data-driven manufacturing optimization and materials discovery can overcome traditional constraints on energy density and performance.

## Key findings
- AI/ML optimization enables discovery of novel LFP compositions
- Manufacturing process optimization reduces cost and improves yield
- Data-driven approach accelerates development cycles
- Sustainability advantages of LFP increasingly central to competitiveness
- Digital manufacturing essential to unlocking LFP potential
