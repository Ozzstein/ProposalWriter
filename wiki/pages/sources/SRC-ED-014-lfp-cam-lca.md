# LCA of LFP Cathode Material Production

**Authors**: Xie et al. (2017)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Life cycle assessment of LFP cathode active material production identifying environmental burden distribution across process steps. The study demonstrates that the synthesis step (precursor formation and calcination) represents 52.93% of total environmental burden, highlighting opportunity for process optimization.

## Key findings
- Synthesis step: 52.93% of total environmental burden
- Identifies thermal processing as major impact driver
- LCA framework for CAM production
- Supports prioritization of electrification in synthesis
- Foundation for circular economy assessments
