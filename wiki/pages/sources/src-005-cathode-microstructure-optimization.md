# Optimizing the Microstructure and Processing Parameters for Lithium Ion Battery Cathodes

**Authors**: Karaki, H. et al. (2022)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Presents a digital manufacturing framework for NMC 622 cathode production that correlates processing parameters to resulting microstructure and electrochemical performance. The authors discuss the framework's adaptability to LFP chemistries, suggesting transferability of the digital approach across different cathode material systems.

## Key findings
- Processing parameters directly control cathode microstructure
- Digital framework enables microstructure prediction from inputs
- Framework demonstrates adaptability across cathode chemistries
- Optimization reduces defects and improves performance consistency
