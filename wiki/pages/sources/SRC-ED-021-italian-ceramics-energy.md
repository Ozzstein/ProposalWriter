# Cogeneration in Italian Ceramic Tile Industry

**Authors**: Branchini et al. (2021)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Research on thermal energy systems in Italian ceramic tile manufacturing, establishing spray drying thermal demand baseline of 0.70-1.10 kWh/kg water evaporated. Provides Italian industrial analog for understanding thermal process requirements in energy-intensive manufacturing.

## Key findings
- Spray dryer thermal demand: 0.70-1.10 kWh/kgH2O
- Italian manufacturing context and data
- Cogeneration feasibility in industrial processes
- Regional benchmark for CAM synthesis thermal requirements
