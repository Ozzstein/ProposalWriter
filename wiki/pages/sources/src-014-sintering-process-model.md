# Mathematical Model and Parameter Analysis of the Sintering Process for Ternary Cathode Materials

**Authors**: Ma, B. et al. (2023)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Presents a comprehensive mathematical model for NMC811 sintering that integrates chemical reaction kinetics, thermal conduction physics, and computational fluid dynamics. The model achieves predictive accuracy within ±10% error margin, demonstrating the feasibility of digitally representing calcination/sintering operations for cathode active material manufacturing.

## Key findings
- Integrated chemical-thermal-fluid dynamics model for sintering
- Model predicts final phase composition and morphology
- ±10% error margin validates model adequacy
- Temperature profile critical to phase evolution
- Model enables process optimization and scale-up
