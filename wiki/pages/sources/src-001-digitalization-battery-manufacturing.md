# Digitalization of Battery Manufacturing - Current Status, Challenges, and Opportunities

**Authors**: Ayerbe, E. et al. (2021)
**Type**: review | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Comprehensive review of digitalization across the lithium-ion battery (LIB) manufacturing chain. The review identifies critical infrastructure gaps preventing widespread adoption of digital manufacturing strategies, including lack of standardized data formats across manufacturing stages, insufficient real-time sensing capabilities, and challenges in integrating multi-scale computational models for process control.

## Key findings
- Lack of standardized data formats across manufacturing stages is a primary barrier to digitalization
- Real-time sensing infrastructure is inadequate for closed-loop process control
- Multi-scale model integration remains technically challenging
- Digital twin concepts applicable across entire LIB value chain
