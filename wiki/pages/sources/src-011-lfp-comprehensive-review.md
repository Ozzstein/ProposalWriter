# Recent Advances in Lithium Iron Phosphate Battery Technology - A Comprehensive Review

**Authors**: Chen, T. et al. (2024)
**Type**: review | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Current 2024 review of LFP cathode technology covering synthesis pathways, manufacturing processes, and performance parameters. The review systematically examines solid-state, hydrothermal, and sol-gel synthesis routes, identifying critical quality parameters and scale-up considerations for each approach.

## Key findings
- Solid-state synthesis enables morphology control
- Hydrothermal methods reduce synthesis temperature and energy
- Sol-gel provides high surface area and phase purity
- Carbon coating and coating materials critical to performance
- Manufacturing route selection impacts cost and performance trade-offs
