# Low-carbon cement manufacturing enabled by electrified calcium looping

**Authors**: Liu, X. et al. (2023)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Demonstrates that electrified calcium looping enables low-carbon cement manufacturing with simultaneous CO2 capture at 90% efficiency. The technology provides a direct analog for electrified lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cathode calcination, showing how industrial process heating can be decarbonized while capturing process-generated CO2.

## Key findings
- Electrified calcium looping achieves 90% CO2 capture during cement production
- Process heat can be supplied via electric resistance or induction heating
- Technology is scalable to industrial cement kiln operations
- Direct analog for LFP cathode active material calcination processes
