# Advances in reactive co-precipitation for high-performance cathodes

**Authors**: Wang, Z. et al. (2023)
**Type**: review | **Quality**: high
**Citations**: 50 in literature

## Summary
Comprehensive review of reactive co-precipitation methods for cathode active material synthesis, documenting industrial-scale practice and identifying critical knowledge gaps. Highlights that industrial scale-up remains largely semi-empirical, with significant opportunities for theoretical modeling and in-line process analytical technology (PAT) development to improve consistency and reduce manufacturing variability.

## Key findings
- Reactive co-precipitation is established industrial precursor synthesis method
- Industrial scale-up practice is predominantly semi-empirical without first-principles models
- Clear research gap in theoretical understanding of precipitation kinetics
- In-line monitoring and PAT systems underdeveloped for production scale
- Process control improvements critical for cost reduction and quality consistency
