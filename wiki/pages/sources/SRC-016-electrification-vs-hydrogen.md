# Electrification or Hydrogen? The Challenge of Decarbonizing Industrial Process Heat

**Authors**: Leicher, J. et al. (2024)
**Type**: review | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Examines the pathways for decarbonizing industrial process heat above 400°C, comparing direct electrification and hydrogen-based approaches. Both technologies are viable decarbonization solutions, with electrification emerging as the favored pathway when coupled with renewable energy sources, particularly for applications requiring sustained high temperatures.

## Key findings
- Direct electrification is technically feasible for industrial process heating >400°C
- Electrification becomes economically favorable with high renewable energy penetration
- Hydrogen remains a viable alternative for specific industrial applications
- Grid decarbonization is critical to realizing emissions benefits of electrified heating
