# Design of an ISO 23247 Compliant Digital Twin for an Automotive Assembly Line

**Authors**: Melo, V. et al. (2024)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Demonstrates ISO 23247-compliant digital twin deployment in an automotive body shop inspection and quality control context. The case study shows practical application of the standard framework to achieve zero-defect manufacturing objectives through real-time quality monitoring and process feedback.

## Key findings
- ISO 23247 framework applicable to automotive assembly line quality control
- Real-time inspection data enables defect prevention
- Digital representation supports process feedback and adjustment
- Standard enables interoperability across quality systems
