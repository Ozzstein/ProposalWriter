# Custom Ultrasonic Spray Pyrolysis Equipment for Scalable Synthesis of LiFePO4

**Authors**: Volobuyeva, V. et al. (2025)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Describes custom-built six-zone programmable spray pyrolysis equipment for LFP/carbon composite synthesis. The work demonstrates how precise control of thermal zones and injection parameters enables tuning of crystallinity and particle morphology, providing process parameters as design variables for optimization.

## Key findings
- Six-zone thermal control enables process parameter precision
- Process parameters directly affect morphology and crystallinity
- Ultrasonic spray enables fine droplet size control
- Programmable equipment enables rapid process exploration
- Carbon composite synthesis in single step
