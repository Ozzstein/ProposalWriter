# Application of Digital Twin Technology in Production Process Optimization of New Energy Battery Packs

**Authors**: Li, C. et al. (2024)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: medium

## Summary
Applies digital twin technology specifically to LFP battery pack assembly line optimization. The paper demonstrates 3D digital representation of production flow, enabling simulation-based forecasting of bottlenecks and optimization of assembly sequences. The work validates DT applicability at the pack integration stage of LFP manufacturing.

## Key findings
- 3D digital representation enables assembly line simulation
- Production flow bottlenecks identifiable through DT simulation
- Assembly sequence optimization reduces cycle time
- DT applicable at pack integration stage for LFP systems
