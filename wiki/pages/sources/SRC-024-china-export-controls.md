# Chinese Export Controls on Cathode Technology

**Authors**: DGAP (2025)
**Type**: policy_document | **Quality**: high

## Summary
German Council on Foreign Relations (DGAP) policy brief analyzing China's July 2025 implementation of export licensing requirements for LFP cathode technology. Documents the strategic implications of Chinese export controls on EU battery manufacturing independence and the urgency of establishing European alternatives to Chinese-controlled supply chains.

## Key findings
- China implements export licensing for LFP cathode technology (July 2025)
- Export controls directly impact non-Chinese battery manufacturers
- EU dependency on Chinese cathode technology threatens strategic autonomy
- Policy brief confirms strategic necessity of EU domestic CAM production
- Technology export controls create competitive disadvantage for EU industry
