# Full-automatic production process of LFP

**Authors**: Yichun Tianzhi (2013)
**Type**: Patent | **Quality**: Medium

## Summary
Chinese patent CN103151525A describing a fully automated LFP (lithium iron phosphate) production process using PLC (programmable logic controller) automation. The approach represents traditional automatic control rather than digital twin integration, with patent expiry approaching 2033.

## Key findings
- PLC-based automation for LFP synthesis
- Reaching end-of-life in terms of patent protection (2033)
- Does not employ digital twin or advanced data-driven control methodologies
- Establishes baseline automation standards in LFP production
