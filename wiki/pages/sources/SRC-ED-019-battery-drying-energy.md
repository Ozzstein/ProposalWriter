# Reducing Energy in Battery Drying Processes

**Authors**: Schütte et al. (2024)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Recent research on near-infrared (NIR) drying technologies for battery material drying processes, quantifying energy savings and emissions reductions. Study projects 2.63 Mt CO2eq annual emissions avoidance by 2030 through widespread adoption of advanced drying technologies.

## Key findings
- Near-infrared drying approach
- 2.63 Mt CO2eq emissions avoidance potential by 2030
- Alternative to conventional thermal drying
- Significant scale-up potential
- Recent research (2024) with forward-looking analysis
