# Energy of current and future LIB cell production

**Authors**: Degen et al. (2023)
**Type**: Paper (Nature Energy) | **Quality**: Very High

## Summary
Landmark research in Nature Energy quantifying lithium-ion battery (LIB) cell production energy requirements with scenario analysis for future improvements. Study establishes LFP cell production at 37.5 kWh/kWh cell, projecting 66% energy reduction potential by 2040 through electrification and process optimization.

## Key findings
- Current LFP: 37.5 kWh/kWh cell produced
- 66% reduction potential by 2040
- Electrification as primary efficiency lever
- 427 citations in Nature Energy
- High-impact journal publication with broad influence
