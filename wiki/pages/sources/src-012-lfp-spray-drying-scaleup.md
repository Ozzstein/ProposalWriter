# LiFePO4 Spray Drying Scale-up and Carbon-cage for Improved Cyclability

**Authors**: Rigamonti, M. et al. (2020)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high

## Summary
Demonstrates successful spray drying scale-up for LFP cathode active material production. The work introduces a carbon-cage coating strategy that significantly improves electrochemical cyclability. Achieves capacity of 161 mAh/g at 0.1C rate with excellent cycle life, validating spray drying as a scalable LFP synthesis route.

## Key findings
- Spray drying enables scalable LFP synthesis
- Carbon-cage coating improves capacity and cycle life
- Capacity retention maintained over extended cycling
- Process parameters control final morphology and performance
- Demonstrated performance metrics exceed baseline LFP specifications
