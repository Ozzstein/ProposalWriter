# Investigating Electrode Calendering and Its Impact on Electrochemical Performance by Means of a New Discrete Element Method Model

**Authors**: Ngandjong, A. et al. (2020)
**Type**: paper | **Quality**: high

## Summary
This is the first key paper demonstrating the ARTISTIC platform's capability to model electrode calendering using discrete element method (DEM). Although the validation was performed on NMC111 chemistry, the study demonstrates a complete digital thread from manufacturing process parameters through to electrochemical performance prediction, establishing the platform's foundational methodology.

## Key findings
- DEM model validated for electrode calendering process
- Digital thread established: manufacturing inputs → microstructure → electrochemical outputs
- Process parameters directly affect porosity, tortuosity, and electrochemical performance
- Platform demonstrates scalability across electrode chemistries
