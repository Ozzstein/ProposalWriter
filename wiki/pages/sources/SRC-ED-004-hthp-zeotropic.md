# High-Temperature Heat Pumps using Zeotropic Working Fluids for Spray Drying

**Authors**: Zühlsdorf et al. (2017)
**Type**: Paper | **Quality**: High

## Summary
Research demonstrating advanced HTHP technology using zeotropic working fluid blends for efficient spray drying operations. The study reports coefficient of performance (COP) of 3.04 at 120°C, achieving 36% reduction in gas consumption compared to conventional thermal systems.

## Key findings
- COP 3.04 at 120°C operating temperature
- 36% reduction in gas energy consumption
- Zeotropic fluid approach improves thermodynamic efficiency
- Applicable to spray drying and other batch thermal processes
- Established technology with proven performance metrics
