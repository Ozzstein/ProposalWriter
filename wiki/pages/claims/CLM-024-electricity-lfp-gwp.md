# Electricity consumption during LFP battery manufacture is the single largest contributor to GWP, accounting for 39.7% of total climate change impact (Lin et al., 2024). Switching to clean electricity is the most impactful single decarbonization intervention — more effective than recycling or chemistry changes.

**Confidence**: 0.90 | **Category**: technical

## Supporting evidence
- [[sources/SRC-ED-015]] — Lin et al. (2024) LCA of LFP battery manufacture attributing 39.7% of GWP to electricity consumption
- [[sources/SRC-ED-020]] — Comparative decarbonization pathway analysis confirming electricity source as dominant lever vs recycling and chemistry alternatives
