# A multi-scale modeling framework spanning DFT/atomistic to plant scale (CALPHAD thermodynamics, rotary kiln CFD, population balance models, DEM for milling, Jander/Avrami reaction kinetics) exists for the individual physics of LFP synthesis, but these models have never been integrated into a unified DT for CAM production.

**Confidence**: 0.88 | **Category**: novelty

## Supporting evidence
- [[sources/SRC-014]] — Multi-scale modeling literature for LFP synthesis including CALPHAD, CFD, population balance, and reaction kinetics models as separate tools
- [[sources/SRC-DT-REPORT]] — DT landscape report confirming that no unified multi-scale DT has been assembled for LFP CAM production
