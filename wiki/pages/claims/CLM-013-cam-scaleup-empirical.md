# Industrial scale-up of battery cathode material synthesis is currently semi-empirical, with a recognized gap in theoretical models and in-line process monitoring for production-scale reactor and kiln control. PAT/QbD frameworks from pharmaceuticals are transferable but have not been implemented for battery CAM.

**Confidence**: 0.88 | **Category**: sota

## Supporting evidence
- [[sources/SRC-028]] — Review of PAT/QbD frameworks in pharmaceuticals and their potential transferability to battery CAM manufacturing
- [[sources/SRC-001]] — Battery manufacturing process control literature confirming semi-empirical scale-up practice
- [[sources/SRC-DT-REPORT]] — DT landscape report documenting absence of PAT implementation for battery CAM production
